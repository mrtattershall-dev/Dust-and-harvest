Our roots

Blackwood born, Oklahoma raised

F&M started the way most good things around here do: a kitchen table, a borrowed vise and a creek that needed fishing.

Our roots run back to Blackwood, to folks who hunted what they ate, fished before church and figured the woods belonged to whoever showed up. We still tie that way. Nothing fancy, just patterns that catch Oklahoma fish in Oklahoma water.

Our fly boxes are cut from Eastern red cedar cleared off local pasture, so a tree that was choking out the prairie ends up riding in your vest pocket.

Not just for fly folks

For whoever loves it out here

You don't need a fly rod to belong at F&M. If you'd rather spend Saturday in a deer stand, on a birding trail or at a campsite by the lake, pull up a stool.

Anglers of every kind

Fly, spin or cane pole. Our soft plastics are poured for bass fishers and our flies work on bluegill as well as trout.

Birders & wildlife watchers

Bald eagles winter along our lakes and rivers. Ask us where to look and bring binoculars.

Hunters & campers

Hats and shirts built to take briars, campfire smoke and a long drive home on a dirt road.

First-timers

Free Saturday casting lessons in the lot out back. Bring the kids. We'll bring the loaner rods.
